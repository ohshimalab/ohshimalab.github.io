国際会議 [The 13th International Conference on Behavioural and Social Computing (BESC 2026)](https://besc-conf.org/2026/) に桑田若菜さん、木下真帆さん、松本美風さんの論文が採録されました。

2026年10月26日～28日にタイ・バンコクで開催されたBESC 2026に参加し、桑田若菜さんが口頭発表、木下真帆さんと松本美風さんがポスター発表をしてきました。


書誌情報は以下の通りです。

- Wakana Kuwata, Ryota Mibayashi, Masanori Tani, Hiroaki Ohshima: "Japanese Handwriting Generation Based on Human Preference Feedback Using Direct Preference Optimization", Proceedings of the 13th International Conference on Behavioural and Social Computing (BESC 2026), October 2026.
- Maho Kinoshita, Wakana Kuwata, Ryota Mibayashi, Hiroaki Ohshima: "Analyzing Atypical Local Features in Museum Artifacts using Generative Image Inpainting", Proceedings of the 13th International Conference on Behavioural and Social Computing (BESC 2026), October 2026.
- Mifu Matsumoto, Yuki Nakanishi, Tomoya Hashiguchi,Hiroaki Ohshima: "Structured Maps for Landmark-Based Route Instructions with LLMs", Proceedings of the 13th International Conference on Behavioural and Social Computing (BESC 2026), October 2026.
